\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}
Astrocytic calcium dynamics can display amplitude, frequency, and mixed
amplitude--frequency modulation as the input changes. The Li--Rinzel model
provides a reduced description of intracellular calcium signalling
\cite{LiRinzel1994}, and its astrocytic dynamics have been studied across these
regimes \cite{DePitta2009}.

% Explain motivation for phenomenological reduction and network simulation.
% State the research question and distinguish the present two-threshold models
% from a separate high-accuracy reproduction of the full LR bifurcation diagram.
Here we investigate continuous three-zone piecewise-affine oscillators as
phenomenological representations of selected Li--Rinzel regimes. We compare
local and global oscillatory behaviour, fits in physical and transformed
coordinates, and simulation times in NEST networks.
