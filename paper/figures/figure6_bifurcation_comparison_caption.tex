Equilibria and periodic-orbit calcium extrema for Li--Rinzel and mixed-coordinate two-threshold PWA models. Columns show AM, FM, and AFM; rows show the reference, PWA with the upper cycle fold constrained, and PWA without a fold constraint. Solid blue and dashed red curves indicate stable and unstable equilibria; the latter include saddles. Filled black and open red circles denote stable and unstable periodic orbits; diamonds denote computed cycle folds. In the lower row the folds are numerical candidates found after fitting. Li--Rinzel cycles are obtained by continuation; PWA cycles use finite forward scans and boundary matching with independent forward or backward integration checks. These searches are not exhaustive. For FM without a fold constraint, an earlier candidate from the archive is shown. The fixed-fold AM fit imposes an orbit crossing both thresholds, which does not reproduce the LR fold orbit's threshold itinerary.
