For each AM, AFM and FM reference, we fit a continuous three-zone PWA field in physical calcium coordinates. The equilibrium at $I=0$, the two inputs $I_1,I_2$ at which the equilibrium crosses the switching lines, and the input $I_*$ of the upper LR cycle fold are fixed. The regional time-scale coefficients and switching thresholds are held at their reference values. Four dependent coefficients enforce the equilibrium constraints; a further coefficient is found numerically together with the flight times by requiring orbit closure and $\log M=0$ at $I_*$. Failure of this numerical solve leads to rejection of the trial, rather than a claim that no solution exists.

We require a stable lower region (node or focus), an unstable middle focus, and a stable upper focus. Independent trial starts use six normalized coefficient offsets sampled uniformly from $[-40,40]$ and four flight times sampled independently log-uniformly between $0.05$ and $50$ s. These are bounded, reference-scaled starting distributions, not uniform sampling of all possible fields. A numerical joint search for a neutral orbit precedes local least-squares fitting. Searches continue until 64 candidates per regime satisfy the numerical screening; the selection reported here uses seeds AM 465, AFM 800 and FM 474. A valid candidate is not necessarily a global minimum.

The joint search uses at most 300 objective evaluations. The subsequent bounded trust-region reflective least-squares fit permits at most 80 evaluations, with \texttt{ftol=xtol=gtol=$10^{-5}$} and a relative finite-difference step of $10^{-3}$. Early termination does not by itself demonstrate global or even high-accuracy local convergence. Bounds on normalized free coefficients are $[-40,40]$; infeasible evaluations use a numerical search barrier and are not reported as valid fitted losses.

For seven fitting inputs, the objective is
\begin{equation}
\sum_i \left[\left(\frac{\delta C_{\min,i}}{0.03\,\mu\mathrm M}\right)^2+\left(\frac{\delta C_{\max,i}}{0.08\,\mu\mathrm M}\right)^2+\left(\frac{\delta T_i}{5\,\mathrm s}\right)^2+\frac{\operatorname{mean}_k(\delta w_{ik}^2)}{0.1^2}\right],
\end{equation}
where $w$ denotes the 64-sample cycle waveform aligned at its maximum and normalized by its amplitude. The last term describes waveform shape; it is not accumulated phase error under a varying input. Calcium positivity and itinerary checks are numerical rejection criteria, not loss terms. Four additional inputs are held out. Cycle search and checks use finite initial guesses, tolerances and sampling, so failure to find an orbit does not establish nonexistence.
