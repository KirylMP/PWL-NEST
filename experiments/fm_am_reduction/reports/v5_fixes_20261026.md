# FM v5 numerical corrections — 2026-10-26

## Scope

The supplied `best_so_far.json` from `fm_full_v4_64`, trial 9, is preserved as
`tests/fixtures/fm_v4_stalled.json` (model fields only). V5 changes the numerical
section policy, parameter coordinates, continuation and audit rules. It retains
the five scientific loss terms, weights 1/10/10/30/30 and the unaveraged
extra-cycle term. It does not introduce period/amplitude fitting.

The original physical model has not been improved merely by rescoring it.
This release repairs the search and its diagnostics; a full server search is
still required. Historical v4 losses and the new v5 losses are not comparable
without rescoring on the same section policy, epsilon and grids.

## Reproduced corrections

At the unchanged fixture parameters, a 65-node moving physical section with
epsilon .001 gives:

| I | PWA stable count | PWA unstable count | LR stable count | LR unstable count |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| .65 | 2.00000230 | 1.00000230 | 1.00000315 | .00000315 |
| .8 | 1.00000315 | .00000315 | 1.00000230 | .00000230 |
| 1.0 | .00000320 | .00000320 | 1.00000240 | 1.00000240 |

Thus the confirmed three-cycle regime is penalized, the small stable cycle at
I=.8 is visible, and the missing cycles at I=1 are retained as fitting errors.
The section at I=.8 is C=.3594120433; the former fixed LR section lay outside
the small candidate orbit's C range. At I=.65 the weighted extra-cycle penalty
is approximately 30, rather than being suppressed by excessive smoothing.
Tiny nonzero counts reflect the smooth positive-part regularization.

A controlled three-I broad-smoothing descent check, using five solver
evaluations plus three recovery probes, reduced the same objective from
116.4525736952 to 116.3396731698. Its regional scaled optimality remained
28664.7 and `converged_stationary` was correctly false. This is a descent
regression, not evidence of scientific convergence.

At exactly the same parameters, three-I objective and epsilon .001, the raw
Jacobian condition number was 1,725,991 and the regional-coordinate condition
number was 619,643. Conditioning improved in this check but remains poor; no
claim is made that the parameter degeneracy has disappeared.

## Remaining scientific question

Numerically trustworthy counts do not determine orbit amplitude, period or
branch geometry. Those remain independent output diagnostics. Finite grids and
multi-section scans also do not prove cycle completeness. A new server run must
be reviewed through its bifurcation plots and structural report, even if it
passes numerical admissibility and stationarity checks.
