# Mixed AFM: fit local HLB2 period

Uses unchanged mixed AFM as seed. Keeps the first region a stable node (`x2 >= 1e-4`) and second an unstable focus, fixed rest and threshold crossings, unrestricted q, no fold or global Poincare fitting. Training minimizes the previous normalized stable waveform/period loss plus `weight*((T_limit-14.388890297911214)/14.388890297911214)^2` for weights 0.001, 0.01 and 0.1 by default. The LR target comes from the local lower Hopf eigenfrequency, not the period of an orbit at finite amplitude. This is a soft penalty, not a hard match.

`period.py` derives T_limit from the normalized piecewise-affine flight-time matching equations at fixed I. The normalized matching is used solely to evaluate the true local cycle period; no matching error is added as an independent fitting objective. Invalid/unresolved local cycles incur a penalty and cannot become the best valid checkpoint. Numerical limits on precision and flight times make nonconvergence possible and do not establish infeasibility. The computation assumes two-region dynamics and uses arbitrary precision; candidate success requires positive crossing speeds, stable log multiplier and a resolved period, but the fast evaluation does not certify the complete itinerary.

After selection, `hlb2_afm/check.py` recomputes the local period at higher precision and checks the two-region itinerary using sampling, while `free_hidden_refine/validate.py` independently evaluates stable trajectories on the same seven training and four held-out inputs. The old model is validated identically. Model parameters and optimizer endpoint success are not proof of global optimality, branch completeness, or neighborhood size; compare only physically admissible sampled candidates and inspect `hlb2_audit.status`. Different weights produce different losses and must be compared through reported errors and period, not raw training loss. Frozen models are read only.
