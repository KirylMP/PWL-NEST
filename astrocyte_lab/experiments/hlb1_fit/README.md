# Refit with subcritical lower focus-focus HLB1

Fit six two-threshold AM/AFM/FM models in physical and mixed coordinates, keeping fixed physical thresholds, rest at I=0 and the two LR crossing inputs. The bottom region is a stable focus, middle is an unstable focus, top is a stable focus. We enforce the lower HLB1 sign analytically. With T0=-2 sqrt(D0) e and omega0=sqrt(D0) sqrt(1-e²), and A=T1/(2 omega1)>0, choose `e = A/sqrt(1+A²) * sigmoid(v)`; then `0<e<1` and `alpha = -e/sqrt(1-e²) + A > 0` for every bounded optimizer variable v. `x[2]=log(e)` is stored in the usual model parameter representation. Positive alpha predicts a local unstable cycle for a generic, transverse, continuous focus-focus HLB1. Its existence on the expected side is checked by the finite lower_hlb boundary search, not inferred solely from optimizer success.

Train on the same 7 stable cycles and independently validate 4 holdout values, preserving earlier waveform/period objectives and FM peak weight 1 (AM/AFM 0). No fold or Poincare loss. q unrestricted. The previous candidate is only an initialization: forcing the HLB1 property can substantially perturb it. Random bounded restarts, finite starting screens and optimizer are not proofs of infeasibility or optimality. Current previous frozen models are never modified. Selected candidates, raw fit logs, DOP853 cycle validation, lower local-cycle audit, and old-model validation are saved under each new run folder. Additional unstable cycles and bifurcation branches away from the lower threshold are not enumerated.
