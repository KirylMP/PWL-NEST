# Lower transition: focus-focus HLB1 and local cycles

For the current physical and mixed no-fold candidates, calculate the one-sided regional traces, determinants and discriminants. If both sides are foci with stable left and unstable right, compute `alpha = T_left/(2 omega_left) + T_right/(2 omega_right)`, `omega=sqrt(4D-T²)/2`, and `exp(pi*alpha)`. The last number is the limiting full return-map slope at the boundary equilibrium; it is not generally the Floquet multiplier of a nonzero cycle. In the standard continuous focus-focus HLB1, alpha<0 corresponds to a small stable cycle and alpha>0 to a small unstable cycle, subject to nondegeneracy and a transverse equilibrium crossing. Source: Simpson & Meiss, https://arxiv.org/abs/nlin/0701036 ; the parameterization fixes the two crossing inputs but broader hypotheses should be verified before a theorem-level claim.

For both signs of selected I offsets, solve the exact PWA boundary matching equations for cycles crossing only the first threshold, check crossing directions and sampled regional itinerary, and independently integrate the closed orbit and its trace integral with DOP853. Report amplitude, period, `log_multiplier` and `stable`. The finite multistart scan can miss cycles, and does not examine large cycles crossing both thresholds, global separatrices, or other branches. Coordinates C are recovered physically; q has no artificial [0,1] bound. Near-zero amplitudes and grazing orbits can be numerically ill-conditioned. LR's lower bifurcation may be governed by different mechanisms.

`--candidate` analyses an individual model; otherwise `--snapshot` and `--physical-run` read the current frozen mixed models and the most recent physical no-fold candidates. The models are only read, with SHA256 recorded. No optimization or parameters are changed.
