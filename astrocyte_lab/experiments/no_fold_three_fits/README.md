# Mixed-coordinate models fitted without folds

Independent fits for K_ER=0.05, 0.075, 0.10. Physical rest, two calcium thresholds and their equilibrium crossing inputs are fixed; regional type constraints are retained. All free dynamical and coordinate parameters are initialized by independent bounded random draws, not by perturbing prior candidates. Bounds and RNG seed are saved in `initialization`. A finite screen discards candidates with nonphysical equilibrium heights and prefers random candidates producing stable trajectories at three training inputs. If no physically admissible sampled candidate produces any such trajectory after `--init-tries` attempts, the trial is marked `random_initialization_failed`. This is a finite screen, not evidence of infeasibility or completeness.

Fit the 64-sample physical C waveform, aligned at its peak and sampled at normalized phase, and period at seven stable-cycle reference inputs. Four other inputs are held out. No fold location, M=1, fold target, fold matching or unstable-cycle search is used. The solver uses a finite-step integrator, a domain penalty and can have nonsmooth residuals or fail to find cycles. Convergence and optimality must be inspected separately from cycle detection.

After selection independent DOP853 stable trajectories are calculated at all 11 reference inputs and compared to the archived fixed-fold models. No global cycle completeness is implied. `baseline_comparison.json`, `validation.json` and `comparison.png` live with model parameters. Prior models are used only as comparison data, not to initialize optimization. User-uploaded prior reference cycles supply the targets.
