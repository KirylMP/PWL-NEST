# High K_ER phenomenological family

The independent physical-C two-threshold models use the AM regional construction (stable focus / unstable focus / stable focus). At each K, the LR rest and both numerically computed equilibrium Hopf crossings (C,I) are exactly imposed. This is an equilibrium-event surrogate: it does not create an actual smooth Hopf bifurcation in PWA. Models are fitted to LR finite forward trajectories at constant I and vector fields, without any cycle-fold or unstable-cycle constraint. Cycle targets require convergence of peaks over the latter half of a finite simulation; this is not a complete enumeration. Candidate trajectory fitting uses fixed-step RK4. Candidates are re-integrated with DOP853 at each training input and checked against RK4; candidates failing this comparison are excluded from the parameter fit. Independent holdout inputs, a resolution study, and cycle completeness analysis are still needed.

After fitting, selected coefficient forms are fit to the candidate coefficients: linear, reciprocal, quadratic and sigmoid. Parameters use t=(K-.105)/.005. Report training and leave-one-out error. These are post-hoc approximations, not a jointly constrained model; they do not preserve rest, event constraints, oscillations, or stability at interpolated K. The quadratic curvature is reported rather than forced. `a`, `b` and both thresholds also change with K and must be modeled before the coefficient fits define a complete system.

`hopf_lyapunov.py` uses exact symbolic derivatives via SymPy and locates a zero of the first Lyapunov coefficient of the upper LR Hopf. The point is a generalized Hopf candidate: neither the second Lyapunov coefficient nor the global fold connection is proved.
