# Two-threshold family over K_ER

Default range K_ER=0.01–0.11 µM, 21 points. C is physical and q uses identity coordinates. LR rest and upper equilibrium Hopf crossing (C,I) are fixed. The lower LR Hopf is fixed only if it exists on the located branch and is ordered before the upper event; otherwise the first model threshold and its crossing input are fitted as effective parameters and are explicitly marked as such in the result. The stable lower region may be a node or focus. The middle and upper regions have unstable and stable focus types. Fixed point root searches and finite input scans are not proofs of completeness.

Models fit finite forward LR trajectories and vector fields, without cycle-fold or unstable-cycle constraints. LR trajectories are sampled at constant input from two initial states and require converged, finite-amplitude oscillations; missing targets are reported as `reference_cycles_unresolved`, not absence of cycles. Training uses a fixed-step RK4 engine; selected candidates are compared with DOP853 on the same inputs. Failing candidates are excluded from coefficient fitting. Independent holdout inputs, a resolution study, and cycle completeness analysis are needed. Optimizer `success` and optimality are reported and a finite candidate does not imply convergence.

Coefficient approximations are post-hoc, using t=(K-.06)/.05. They do not preserve rest, event constraints, oscillations, or stability between K values. There may be missing K values, construction changes, parameter nonidentifiability and disconnected optimizer branches. The quadratic curvature is reported rather than forced. `a`, `b` and both thresholds also vary with K; fitting the displayed coefficients alone does not define a complete family.

`hopf_lyapunov.py` uses exact symbolic derivatives via SymPy and locates a zero of the first Lyapunov coefficient of the upper LR Hopf. The point is a generalized Hopf candidate: neither the second Lyapunov coefficient nor the global fold connection is proved.
