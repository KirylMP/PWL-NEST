# Final two-threshold fits at three K values

K_ER = 0.05, 0.075, 0.10. Physical C and identity q, fixed LR rest and the two LR equilibrium threshold crossings. Continuous PWA has a stable lower node or focus, unstable middle focus and stable upper focus. The fixed fold input is the numerically continued LR fold. The model fold is forced to cross both thresholds: the source AM fold crosses only the upper threshold, but a nondegenerate fold in I on a fixed one-threshold itinerary is incompatible with the present continuous affine structure. Thus extrema at the fold may differ.

The objective fits period and phase-aligned C waveform of the *stable* cycles; no Poincare return loss is used. Seven of 11 inputs train and four are held out; waveform phase zero is the calcium peak and period is separate. The fold constraint uses exact affine boundary matching and M=1. Each accepted fold undergoes numerical nondegeneracy checks, sampled region checks and independent DOP853 closure. `validate.py` repeats stable trajectories and tries local stable and unstable cycles on both sides of the fold. A missing orbit in its finite scan is unresolved, not proof of nonexistence. This does not check global cycle completeness; near-fold cycles are checked by boundary matching and sampling, not independent IVP integration. Reference cycles are finite trajectories.

All intermediate failures and logs stay in the run directory; no accepted model is overwritten. Winners are selected by training loss after fold verification and training-cycle detection. Compare errors and missed cycles in validation.json, including held-out inputs. Solver success and finite differences should be inspected. These models do not match all details of LR or ensure oscillations outside the sampled inputs.
